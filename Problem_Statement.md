## 4. Problem Statement

Many students prepare for placements using different websites for aptitude practice, coding, resume building, and interview preparation. Since these resources are scattered across multiple platforms, students often find it difficult to monitor their progress, identify weak areas, and receive personalized feedback.

This project aims to provide a single AI-powered platform where students can practice aptitude questions, coding challenges, mock interviews, and resume analysis. Artificial Intelligence will evaluate resumes and interview responses, helping students improve their skills before attending real placement drives.
